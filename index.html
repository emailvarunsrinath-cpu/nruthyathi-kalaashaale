About Us

Founded by Smt. Anusha Varun, Nruthyathi Kalaashaale is a premier center for Bharatanatyam, dedicated to preserving the divinity and technical purity of this ancient art form.

With over 500 students and a core team of 3 trustees, the institution serves as a sanctuary for those looking to master the rhythmic intricacies and expressive nuances of classical dance.
