Legacy of Excellence

Artistic Devotion

Founded by Smt. Anusha Varun, Nruthyathi Kalaashaale nurtures over 500 students, blending the technical precision of the Vasundhara style with deep spiritual expression.

Rooted in tradition and shaped by decades of disciplined practice, our institute offers a structured journey — from a student's first steps in rhythm to the milestone of a solo Rangapravesha — nurturing both technical mastery and inner devotion.
