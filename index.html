About Us

Founded by Smt. Anusha Varun, Nruthyathi Kalaashaale is dedicated to preserving and promoting the rich heritage of Bharatanatyam.

With around 500 students and 3 trustees, our institution serves as a vibrant art center, fostering excellence in classical dance through discipline, devotion, and technical mastery.
