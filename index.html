About Us

Founded by Smt. Anusha Varun, Nruthyathi Kalaashaale is a dedicated center for Bharatanatyam. We provide a nurturing environment for over 500 students to master the technical and expressive elements of this sacred art form.

Our institution focuses on the Vasuadhara style, ensuring that every disciple graduates with a deep understanding of rhythm, grace, and theatrical presence.
