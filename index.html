Legacy of Smt. Anusha Varun

Nruthyathi Kalaashaale is dedicated to preserving the pristine divinity of Bharatanatyam. Under the guidance of Smt. Anusha Varun, we focus on both the theoretical depth and the rhythmic precision of the art form.

Rangavidhipraveshika

The Rangavidhipraveshika is a momentous milestone in a dancer's journey—marking their formal solo debut on stage. It is the culmination of years of rigorous training, discipline, and devotion.
