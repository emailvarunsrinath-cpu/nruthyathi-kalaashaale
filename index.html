About Us

Founded by Smt. Anusha Varun, Nruthyathi Kalaashaale is dedicated to the preservation of the traditional Vasundhara style of Bharatanatyam. With approximately 500 students, our art center fosters technical excellence and spiritual growth.

Our trustees and faculty work tirelessly to provide a platform for classical dance enthusiasts to reach their full potential.
