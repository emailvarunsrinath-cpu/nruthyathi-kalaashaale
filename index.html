About Us

Founded by Smt. Anusha Varun, Nruthyathi Kalaashaale is a temple of Bharatanatyam. We nurture approximately 500 students, guiding them through the Vasundhara style with discipline and grace.

Our mission is to preserve the technical purity of classical dance while fostering a deep spiritual connection in every disciple.
